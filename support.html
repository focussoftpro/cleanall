Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App, go to the Pro page, and tap "Restore Purchase" using the same App Store account used for the original purchase.

Why are my deleted photos still visible?

Deleted photos are moved to the iOS Recently Deleted album first. They are permanently removed after 30 days, or when you empty Recently Deleted manually.

Why did a scan show no results?

Make sure you have granted the App permission to access Photos, Contacts, and Calendar in iOS Settings. Scanning requires those permissions to analyze your library.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. In iOS Settings, tap your name → Subscriptions, select CleanAll Smart, and tap "Cancel Subscription". You can also cancel at any time at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
