Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CleanAll Smart ("the App"), provided by FocusSoft ("we", "our"), handles your information. We have designed the App to be private by default.

1. Data Processing Is Local

All scanning and analysis — duplicate and similar photo detection, video sorting, contact merging, and calendar cleanup — is performed entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos, videos, contacts, or calendar data anywhere.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, record, or process any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not create an account or require registration.
  • Does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that collect personal data.
  • Does not show targeted advertising and contains no ad SDKs.
  • Does not access your photo library, contacts, or calendar except with your explicit permission, and only when you start a scan.

3. Permissions

When you grant access to your Photos, Contacts, or Calendar, the App reads that data only for the local analysis you request. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Deletions

Photos you choose to delete are moved to the iOS Recently Deleted album, from which they can be recovered for 30 days. The App never permanently erases your data beyond what you explicitly confirm.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy practices govern purchase processing.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us
